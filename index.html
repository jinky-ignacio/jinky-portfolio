Proof of Thinking

The work behind the interface.

A polished screen is not enough. The real proof is how the workflow was understood, structured, controlled, and converted into repeatable outputs.

01

Find the friction

Identify repeated encoding, disconnected files, unclear ownership, and weak controls.

02

Map the source of truth

Define the information that must drive dates, quantities, logic, dashboards, and reports.

03

Design the operating logic

Translate field rules, project requirements, relationships, validations, and outputs into a system.

04

Test against reality

Refine the system around actual use, actual constraints, and the way project teams work.

Design Decision LogREV. 04
Decision 01

Activity data should be encoded once and reused across the Gantt, PDM, progress, and reports.

Decision 02

Project dates and relationship logic should control schedule outputs instead of manual visual editing.

Decision 03

Engineering assumptions should remain visible and traceable instead of being hidden inside formulas.

Decision 04

Formal reports must come from saved project data so printed outputs match the working system.
