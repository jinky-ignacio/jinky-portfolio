About Me

I understand the work because I have done the work.

Before building digital systems, I spent more than a decade coordinating, monitoring, inspecting, documenting, and reporting construction projects.

That background shapes how I design. I focus on clarity, practical workflows, traceable information, and outputs that people can actually use—not technology for technology's sake.

Today, I am bringing together civil engineering, project coordination, Excel expertise, workflow design, AI, and software development to create better tools for construction teams.
