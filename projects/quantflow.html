The Problem

Why this system needed to exist.

Quantity estimation can become fragmented across multiple workbooks, manual calculations, and separate summaries. Repetitive encoding and hidden assumptions make estimates difficult to review and maintain.

The Solution

A connected, practical workflow.

QuantFlow organizes member inputs, reinforcement rules, concrete quantities, cutting requirements, engineering summaries, BOQ preparation, and cost analysis into one structured workflow.

Member-Based Inputs

Separate workflows for footings, wall footings, columns, beams, and slabs.

Concrete Quantities

Transparent volume calculations and engineering summaries.

Rebar Engine

Bar definitions, stock lengths, cutting lists, weights, and wastage tracking.

Optimization

Practical allocation logic for stock bars, offcuts, and purchase requirements.

BOQ & Cost Analysis

Structured outputs that support bidding and cost documentation.

Traceable Standards

Visible assumptions, rules, material properties, and configurable defaults.
