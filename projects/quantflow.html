The Problem

Why this system needed to exist.

Quantity estimation can become fragmented across worksheets, manual calculations, and separate summaries. Repetitive encoding, hidden assumptions, inconsistent formulas, and weak traceability make estimates harder to review and maintain.

The Solution

A connected, practical workflow.

QuantFlow organizes structural quantity inputs, reinforcement rules, concrete computations, cutting requirements, engineering summaries, BOQ preparation, and cost-analysis outputs into one structured estimating workflow.

Member-Based Inputs

Separate workflows for footings, wall footings, columns, beams, and slabs.

Concrete Quantities

Transparent calculations for member volumes and engineering summaries.

Rebar Engine

Bar definitions, stock lengths, cutting lists, weight computation, and wastage tracking.

Optimization

Practical allocation logic for stock bars, offcuts, and purchase requirements.

BOQ & Cost Analysis

Structured quantity outputs that support bid and cost documentation.

Traceable Standards

Visible assumptions, construction rules, material properties, and configurable defaults.
