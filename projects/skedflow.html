The Problem

Why this system needed to exist.

Construction schedules are often split across spreadsheets, manually drawn PDM networks, static charts, and separate report files. Every change creates more rework, more duplication, and more opportunities for inconsistencies.

The Solution

A connected, practical workflow.

SkedFlow Pro brings activity data, durations, relationships, dates, weights, project details, and formal outputs into one offline application.

Activity Register

A structured source of truth for activities, quantities, durations, dates, relationships, and weights.

Tender Gantt

A monthly schedule presentation with weighted progress and management-ready output.

PDM Network

Dependency visualization for FS, SS, FF, and SF relationships, including lag and float.

Critical Path

Logic that identifies the activities directly affecting project completion.

BOQ Import

Controlled mapping of bill-of-quantity data into the project planning workflow.

Reports & Backup

Formal printable outputs and reliable project-data export and restoration.
