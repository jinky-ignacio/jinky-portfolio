The Problem

Why this system needed to exist.

Consistent content production becomes difficult when brand rules, ideas, prompts, schedules, assets, captions, and production steps are stored in different places.

The Solution

A connected, practical workflow.

BrainyMom OS connects planning, branding, prompt libraries, asset management, production status, captions, products, and automation into one repeatable workflow.

Frozen visual, messaging, and content standards for consistent output.

Content Planner

A structured calendar for multiple recurring series and formats.

Prompt Libraries

Reusable controlled instructions for images, reels, captions, and carousels.

Asset Management

A searchable image library and organized file workflow.

Production Status

Clear movement from pending content through review and publishing.

Automation Architecture

A workflow designed for future integration with Sheets, Drive, and automation tools.
